# Documentation

Here I collect the stories and the documentation for my projects. Every section has its own entry page with further chapters.

If there is one common thread running through all of it, it is this: Netsnek defines itself through working with others. Almost every project here came about together, with Nico, with Momo, with Andreas, with Felix and Tobias from die Walthergruppe or with the Linuxtag team around Rene. Anyone can build alone. The interesting things happen in between.

## Sections

- [PhotonQ](/docs/photonq): How we built PhotonQ with die Walthergruppe, including the quantum documentation from the project, from OpenQASM to measurement-based quantum computing.
- [Jaen](/docs/jaen): The CMS project I develop with Nico. Together we are trying to bring content management into the 21st century. This site runs on it.
- [Emailwerk](/docs/emailwerk): My self-hosted transactional mail service with templates, multi-provider sending and a built-in admin interface.
- [qtamp](/docs/qtamp): My Qt-native music player that runs original Winamp skins, classic as well as modern, the latter through a complete Maki VM.
- [Linux on Apple Silicon](/docs/linux): My Asahi Linux setup on the MacBook Pro and my own RPM repository rpm.netsnek.com.
- [Security](/docs/security): How I work with hardware keys and qualified electronic signatures, from deterministic YubiKey keys to the OIDC login via ID Austria.
- [Linuxtage](/docs/linuxtage): My talks from community events, and how I help bring the Linuxtage to Carinthia.
- [Internships](/docs/interns): Stories about people who did an internship with me, and their projects.
- [LED art](/docs/ledart): What I built with Andreas in LED art and what software people can learn from technicians.
- [Bäckerherz](/docs/baeckerherz): How Momo and I supported the baked goods delivery service Bäckerherz on the software and sales side, why startups are a numbers game in the end and what we learned about motivation.
