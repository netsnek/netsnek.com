# Linuxtage

Here Florian Kleber collects his talks and slides from community events. The starting point is the Grazer Linuxtage, an annual open-source conference at Graz University of Technology. The event's program archive goes back to 2003, and recordings from many years are freely available online, among other places on media.ccc.de.

This section grows bit by bit. Each talk gets its own page with a short description and the accompanying materials.

## Talks

- [Colorpedia](/docs/linuxtage/colorpedia). A talk about colorpedia.org, the color reference by Netsnek e.U., given at the Grazer Linuxtage.
