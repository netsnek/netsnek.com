# LED Art

This section collects LED art projects that Florian Kleber built together with Andreas Prix.

Andreas passed away in early 2026. These pages remember him and the work we did together. They are meant to record what we built side by side and to preserve the joy that lives in these projects.

The individual project entries will follow once the material has been prepared.
