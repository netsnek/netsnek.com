## What each image shows

- **photonq.jpg** — Photograph of a photonic laboratory bench: fibre couplers, orange mounts,
  half-wave plates and beam-splitter optics on an optical breadboard.
- **jaen.jpg** — Responsive web-design mockup: a client website shown on laptop, tablet and phone.
- **qtamp.jpg** — Screenshot of the qtamp player with main window, equalizer and playlist controls.
- **security.jpg** — Illustration: a balance scale weighing a checklist against a threat/malware icon.
- **linuxtage.jpg** — The Carinthian Linuxday CLD25 key visual: pixel-art Tux next to a computer
  showing a "RESTART" screen under a "LINUXTAG" sign.
- **interns.jpg** — Photograph of two people working at a flipchart during a workshop session.
- **baeckerherz.jpg** — Bäckerherz marketing image: a basket of fresh bread and rolls with the
  claim "Wir liefern täglich frisches Frühstück direkt an deine Haustür!".
- **docs.jpg** — The netsnek platform UI shown on a laptop and a tablet.
- **carinthian-tux.svg** — Tux, the Linux mascot, sitting and holding the Carinthian coat of
  arms (three black lions on gold beside the red-white-red bar). 83 paths, viewBox in
  millimetres, no raster data embedded.

## Sections deliberately left without an image

- **emailwerk** — No honest image was available. The repository's own assets contain nothing
  that depicts emailwerk, and emailwerk was not among the permitted external sources.
- **linux** — Same reason. Nothing in the repository depicts this topic. The Tux artwork from
  carinthianlinuxday.at was not reused here, because the permission given covers the
  Linuxtage section, not a general Linux section.
- **ledart** — `https://eiergeier.at/` responds (HTTP 200), but it could not be confirmed as
  Andreas Prix's site and it does not show LED work. The site is a shop page last updated
  14 April 2005 ("Copyright © 2000 EIERGEIER", Vienna, Löwengasse 14) offering DPSS laser
  projectors and a DMX512 decoder. The strings "Prix" and "Andreas" do not occur anywhere on
  the pages retrieved (`/`, `/laser.htm`, `/dmx512.htm`, `/laslink.htm`), and there is no
  memorial content. Rather than present laser-shop material as LED art, no image was taken.
